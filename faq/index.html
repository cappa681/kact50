Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers for passengers booking a transfer between Kayseri Airport and Cappadocia.

Kayseri Airport transfer questions

How much is the shared shuttle?

The shared shuttle is €15 per passenger on the routes listed on this site.

How much is a private transfer?

Private Vito is €90 per way for up to 5 passengers. Private Sprinter is €110 per way for up to 16 passengers.

Do you take passengers directly to their hotel?

Arrival transfers take passengers to the hotel listed in the booking. Return transfers collect passengers from their hotel for Kayseri Airport.

Can I book a return transfer?

Yes. Return transfers from Cappadocia hotels to Kayseri Airport are available.

What details should I have ready?

Have your flight details, passenger count and full accommodation name ready when booking.
