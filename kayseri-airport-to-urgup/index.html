Shared Shuttle to Ürgüp — €15 per Person

The shared shuttle is a practical option for solo travellers, couples and passengers who want to keep the transfer cost lower.

You travel with other confirmed passengers and are taken to the Ürgüp hotel listed in your booking. Several hotels can be served during the same journey, so the total travel time may be longer than a private transfer.

Private Transfer to Ürgüp

A private transfer reserves the vehicle for your booking only, so there are no hotel stops for unrelated passengers.

Private Vito — €90 / way

A practical choice for couples, families and smaller groups who prefer a more direct journey after arriving at Kayseri Airport.

Private Sprinter — €110 / way

Suitable for larger groups who want to travel together with their luggage in one vehicle.

Ürgüp arrival planning

Ürgüp accommodation is spread across different parts of town. Use the full hotel name shown on your confirmation so the transfer is arranged for the correct property.

If you have a fixed dinner or evening reservation, avoid placing it too close to your flight arrival because baggage collection and shared-hotel stops can add time.

Returning to Kayseri Airport?

Return transfers are available from Ürgüp to Kayseri Airport.

We collect you from your hotel according to the pickup time confirmed for your departure flight.

With a shared shuttle, other hotel pickups may be part of the route. With a private transfer, the vehicle is reserved for your party.

Provide your departure flight number, flight time, hotel name and passenger count when booking the return journey.
